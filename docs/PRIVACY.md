# Privacy Policy

**Doomscroll4**
Last updated: March 15, 2026

## What Doomscroll4 does

Doomscroll4 is a Chrome extension that filters your social media feed using AI classification. It analyzes posts in your feed and categorizes them as relevant, borderline, or off-topic based on your preferences.

## Data we collect

### Data stored locally on your device
- **Your profile preferences**: interests, goal, topics to avoid, and platform settings. Stored in Chrome sync storage and never sent to any third party.
- **Daily usage stats**: count of posts shown, blurred, and hidden. Stored locally and reset daily.
- **Anonymous user ID**: a randomly generated identifier (UUID) used solely for rate limiting. This ID is not linked to your identity.

### Data sent to our server
- **Post text**: the text content of posts in your feed is sent to our classification API to determine relevance. Post text is processed in real time and is **not stored** on our servers.
- **Your profile preferences**: sent alongside post text to personalize classification. **Not stored** on our servers.

## Data we do NOT collect
- We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.
- We do not collect your browsing history.
- We do not collect or store the content of posts after classification.
- We do not use cookies or tracking pixels.
- We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
- We do not run analytics or telemetry.

## Third-party services
Post text is processed using Anthropic's Claude API for classification. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to their processing of this data. No other third-party services are used.

## Data retention
We do not retain post text or user profile data on our servers. Rate limit counters (anonymous UUID + daily request count) are stored temporarily and reset daily.

## Your rights
You can delete all locally stored data at any time by removing the Doomscroll4 extension from Chrome.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as the product evolves. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
